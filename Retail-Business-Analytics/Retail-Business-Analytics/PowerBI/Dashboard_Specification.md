# Power BI Dashboard Specification

## Page 1 — Executive Overview
KPI cards: Revenue, Profit, Orders, Customers, Average Order Value.
Visuals: Monthly Revenue, Revenue by Category, Revenue by Region, Top 10 Products.

## Page 2 — Product Analysis
Revenue by Product, Quantity Sold, Profit Margin by Category.
Slicers: Date, Category, Region.

## Page 3 — Customer Analysis
Top Customers, Customer Revenue, Orders by Customer.

## Page 4 — Regional Analysis
Revenue by Store, Profit by Store, Revenue trend by Region.
