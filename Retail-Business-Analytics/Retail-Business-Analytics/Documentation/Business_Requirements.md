# Business Requirements
## Objective
Create a centralized BI solution for monitoring sales performance.

## Stakeholders
Senior Management, Sales Manager, Product Manager, Regional Managers, Data/Business Analyst.

## Functional Requirements
- FR01: Display total revenue.
- FR02: Display total orders and customers.
- FR03: Filter by date.
- FR04: Filter by region and store.
- FR05: Rank products by revenue.
- FR06: Analyse category performance.
- FR07: Analyse customer performance.
- FR08: Show revenue trends over time.
- FR09: Display profit and profit margin.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- Consistent KPI definitions.
- Easy-to-understand dashboard.
- Efficient loading.
- Authorized access to business data.

## Proposed Solution
Standardize the sales dataset and connect it to Power BI with reusable DAX measures and interactive dashboards.
