# User Stories

US01 — As a sales manager, I want to view revenue by region so that I can identify underperforming regions.
Acceptance: region filter works and revenue updates with date selection.

US02 — As a product manager, I want to identify top and low-performing products so that I can make pricing and promotion decisions.
Acceptance: products can be ranked and filtered by category.

US03 — As a senior manager, I want to see monthly revenue trends so that I can monitor performance.
Acceptance: monthly trend and date filtering are available.

US04 — As a regional manager, I want to compare store performance so that I can identify operational improvement opportunities.
Acceptance: stores can be compared by revenue and profit.
